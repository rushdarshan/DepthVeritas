It rules out from-scratch training of large diffusion/LLM/ViT-g models, full fine-tunes of 7B+ LLMs at 16-bit, and multi-GPU-only pipelines. It is comfortable for: inference of foundation models, LoRA/adapter fine-tuning, small/tiny model variants (≤ ~300M params fp16, or 4-bit 7B), self-supervised feature probing, and distillation. All candidates below are chosen to live inside that envelope with batch-size / precision tricks (fp16/bf16, gradient checkpointing, grad accumulation, 4-bit).

Depth Anything V2 — the strongest reproduce‑and‑extend choice

Monocular depth estimation foundation model from ByteDance and HKU (Lihe Yang et al., NeurIPS 2024). It ships a family from 25M (Small) to 1.3B (Giant) — the Small and Base checkpoints run and even fine-tune inside 6 GB. The training recipe (synthetic-only labels + large-scale pseudo-labeled distillation) is fully documented, the DINOv2 backbone + DPT head is standard, and evaluation datasets (NYUv2, KITTI, DIODE) are public and small enough for a laptop.

Why it wins on your box
  • Small (ViT-S) inference ≈ 1–2 GB; fine-tuning the head/LoRA fits 6 GB with fp16 + grad accumulation.
  • Pretrained weights removes the 595K-image self-training cost — you extend, not retrain the teacher.
  • Datasets are directory-of-images small; no distributed I/O.
  • Deterministic metric (AbsRel, δ1) → clean, publishable ablations.
Extensions with real paper potential
  • Temporal / video consistency head — V2 is per-frame; flicker is an open problem.
  • Metric depth on a novel domain (endoscopy, drone, underwater) via light fine-tune.
  • Edge distillation: distill Small→MobileViT for real-time on-device.
  • Uncertainty / calibration head — the paper gives none.
  • Fuse with SAM 2 masks for object-level metric depth.

Method & sourcing

Sources. Only official lab outputs: arXiv abstracts (IDs verified live in-browser), official GitHub orgs, and official project pages. No blogs, Medium, Kaggle, or coursework. Venues cross-checked (NeurIPS/ICLR/ICML/CVPR).

Screen. (1) major industrial lab authorship, (2) official code, (3) pretrained weights available, (4) public datasets, (5) single-GPU feasible ≤6 GB with standard tricks, (6) 2–8 wk reproduction, (7) explicit extension surface.

Excluded for VRAM. Raw Stable Diffusion training, ViT-g/DINOv2-giant training, 7B+ full FT at 16-bit, SAM full retrain, any multi-GPU-only recipe. Where a model family exists, the small/tiny variant is the target.
